## Notes

- The cube grid is `G=11` with CDF-uniform Gaussian u-axis (10 cells per axis, 1000 cells total).
- The dd_k3 strict FP grids store `P_strict` of shape (11,11,11); the filename suffix `g{...}_t{...}` encodes (gamma, tau), not grid size.
- The trilinear interpolant has piecewise-constant mixed partials, so its grad=0 roots inside a cell are rare; nonetheless trilinear contributes a non-trivial fraction of detections (40/40 at smooth tau=0.2; 60/140 and 32/134 at kinky tau=1).
- The quadratic Hermite fallback adds isotropic curvature terms `c_p eta_p(1-eta_p)` whose `c_p` is the second-difference of trilinear face averages across the adjacent cells. This recovers the additional cusp-inducing interior critical points missed by the strict trilinear pass.
- All accepted critical points satisfy `|grad P_hat| < 1e-8` (in cell-local eta coordinates).
- Cross-check: the cheby_cusps figure (`cheby_cusps/figs/fig2_grad_critical.png`) found 64 critical points by scanning an 80x80 grid on the slice `u1=0` at (gamma=100, tau=1). The present detector finds 140 critical points over the whole cube at the same parameters, consistent with that picture (many cusps).
- All critical p_c values lie in (0, 1), centred near 0.5; tau=1 spreads them out to ~[0.044, 0.956]; tau=0.2 keeps them tight in [0.339, 0.661].
- The g100 and g1000 cases at the same tau yield very similar p_c distributions, confirming the cusps are intrinsic to the equilibrium geometry rather than artefacts of gamma.
