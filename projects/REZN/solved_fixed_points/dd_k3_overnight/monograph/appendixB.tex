\chapter{Glossary}
\begin{description}\itemsep2pt
  \item[REE] rational-expectations equilibrium: price function such
    that demands conditioned on (own signal, price) clear at every
    signal profile.
  \item[FR / PR] fully / partially revealing price function.
  \item[deficit] $1 - R^2$ of $\operatorname{logit}P$ on the pooled
    statistic (with stated state-measure); 0 under FR.
  \item[co-area integral] $\int_{\{P=p\}} f/|\nabla P|\, d\sigma$ ---
    the correct level-set evidence measure.
  \item[\strict{}] the bare co-area operator: explicit contours +
    $1/|\nabla P|$, no kernel.
  \item[kernel band] Gaussian-smoothed indicator
    $\int K_h(P - p) f\, du$; consistent quadrature of the co-area
    integral as $h \to 0$.
  \item[central path / joint limit] $h \to 0$ together with
    $\Delta x \to 0$, $\Delta x \ll h$ --- the regime where the kernel
    is both consistent and smooth.
  \item[lookup] $\mu(p, u_k)$: Bayes posterior of $v = 1$ given own
    signal and the price level set.
  \item[POU] partition of unity across the two sweep directions of the
    level-set integral.
  \item[Morse-critical price] $p_c = P(u^*)$ with $\nabla P(u^*) = 0$:
    location of the $C^0$-not-$C^1$ kink in the evidence and of the
    lookup cusps.
  \item[$\Fw$] density-weighted residual
    $\max |\Phi(P) - P| \cdot \prod_i \bar f(u_i)$ --- the economically
    meaningful convergence metric on unbounded grids.
  \item[trivial branch] $P \equiv \tfrac12$: an exact fixed point at
    all parameters; the uninformative pooled-prior equilibrium.
  \item[best iterate] the lowest-residual point of a non-converging
    iteration --- reported as such, never as a fixed point.
  \item[DD / QD] double-double (32 digits) / quad-double (64 digits)
    arithmetic.
\end{description}
