\chapter{Design principles}

The recommendation reconciles two facts that pull in opposite
directions:
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
  \item The \emph{bare} \strict{} operator is the unbiased definition
        of the equilibrium (no auxiliary parameter at all) --- but it is
        $C^0$-not-$C^1$ at Morse-critical prices and therefore cannot
        be nailed at high resolution by any Newton-type method
        (Part II theorem; Findings 9, 17; Part VI plateaus).
  \item The \emph{consistent kernel} co-area operator is $C^\infty$ and
        nails the equilibrium to $10^{-14}$ --- but carries the
        bandwidth $h$, which must be demonstrated (not assumed) to be a
        pure convergence knob.
\end{enumerate}
The resolution: \textbf{solve with the kernel in the joint central-path
limit; certify against \strict{} at matched tolerance; report the
$h$-ladder as part of the result}. The kernel is then never trusted ---
it is verified, cell by cell.

\chapter{The protocol, step by step}

\section{Step 0: coordinates and grids}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
  \item $u$-grid: uniform private log-odds $\xi = \tanh(\tau u/2)$,
        $\xi_{\max} = 0.85$ (auto-scaling $\pm 5\sigma$; no hard
        truncation). $G$ per the accuracy target below.
  \item $p$-grid: uniform public log-odds $\Lpub$, bootstrap from the
        cube CDF, antisymmetrized by construction; refreshed during
        warm-up only (iteration 5), frozen afterwards.
  \item Weights: trapezoid in $u$; density weight
        $W_3 = \prod_i \bar f(u_i)$ cached for the residual metric.
\end{itemize}

\section{Step 1: continuation ladder (the branch certificate)}
Start at $\tau_0 = 10^{-3}$ from the analytic additive solution
$P = \sigma(\tau \sum_i u_i)$ (Method C v2 closed form; exact there to
$10^{-10}$). March $\tau$ upward in log steps of $\le 5\%$, each cell
warm-started from its predecessor, recording per cell: $\Fw$ best,
symmetry errors, price range, wall time. This ladder \emph{is} the
proof of branch identity: a connected continuation path from the
analytic limit to the target cell. Never accept a cold-started
high-$\tau$ fixed point --- the trivial branch is exact and
attracting.

\section{Step 2: solve each cell with the consistent kernel, joint limit}
At each $(\gamma, \tau)$ target:
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
  \item Solve at $(G_1, h_1)$ with the Gaussian-band co-area operator,
        Anderson or Picard, to its floor; require the floor to be at
        arithmetic level for the precision in use.
  \item Refine along the central path: $(G_{k+1}, h_{k+1}) =
        (\lceil 1.4 G_k \rceil,\; h_k / 1.4)$ keeping
        $h \gtrsim 3\Delta\xi$ always, warm-starting each rung by
        interpolation. Three rungs minimum.
  \item Convergence claim: economic quantities (deficit, slope,
        $P$-range) must agree across the last two rungs to the target
        tolerance, demonstrating $h$-invariance. \emph{The ladder
        table, not a single number, is the result.}
  \item Symmetry projection (permutation + sign-flip) every iteration;
        symmetry errors reported at arithmetic level.
  \item Precision: float64 until the rung floor reaches $10^{-13}$;
        double-double beyond. (QD/F128 retired: no benefit at these
        floors.)
\end{enumerate}

\section{Step 3: the \strict{} cross-check (the unbiasedness certificate)}
At the finest rung, evaluate the \emph{bare} \strict{} residual at the
kernel solution. Accept if
$|F_{\rm strict}|_w \le C \cdot (\text{kernel-rung truncation
estimate})$ with $C \approx 3$. This certifies that the kernel solution
is a \strict{} near-fixed-point to the accuracy the bare operator can
measure --- the kernel never substitutes its own notion of equilibrium.

\section{Step 4: extract the lookup with pipeline v5}
On the certified surface: \texttt{fix\_fallback} $\to$ internal
$\mu$-based cusp detector $\to$ degree-6 per-segment Chebyshev $\to$
PCHIP monotone tails with $\mu(\epsilon) = 0$, $\mu(1-\epsilon) = 1$.
Certify per cell: in-support max error vs the cleaned co-area lookup at
machine epsilon; 11/11 monotone slices on a dense grid; boundary
conditions met. (Demonstrated at 36/36 cells, 7 seconds total.)

\section{Step 5: the validation battery (all five, every cell)}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
  \item \textbf{Symmetry}: permutation, sign-flip, lookup mirror at
        arithmetic level.
  \item \textbf{Monotonicity}: $P$ axis-monotone; $\mu$ monotone in $p$
        per slice (dense check).
  \item \textbf{Boundary conditions}: lookup tails; $P(\mathbf{0}) =
        \tfrac12$ exactly.
  \item \textbf{Operator cross-check}: Step 3 inequality, plus the
        kernel $h$-ladder invariance table.
  \item \textbf{Measure robustness}: report the deficit under at least
        two state weightings (uniform and signal-weighted); the
        $\sim 0.12$ measure spread is part of the honest result, the
        price \emph{surface} being the invariant object.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Step 6: reporting standard}
Every published cell carries: the continuation provenance (ladder
path), the final rung $(G, h, \text{precision})$, the kernel-ladder
invariance table, the \strict{} cross-check value, best-iterate vs
converged status, the symmetry residuals, and the deficit with its
measure convention. Nothing else counts as a result.

\chapter{Open problems}

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
  \item \textbf{Analytic Jacobian through the kernel co-area operator}
        ($\sim 200$ LOC chain rule): would replace finite-difference
        Newton noise and likely extend deep convergence to higher
        $\tau$ at fixed cost.
  \item \textbf{Pseudo-arclength continuation} past the K=2 fold at
        $\tau \approx 0.79$ and a systematic K=3 fold map: the branch
        structure above $\tau \sim 1$ is mapped only along ladders, not
        in the $(\gamma, \tau)$ plane.
  \item \textbf{The 5 corner outlier cells} of the v5 certification
        ($10^{-2}$ max): localized fallback-smoothing artifacts at
        extreme parameter corners; a targeted $G_p$ refinement there is
        unfinished.
  \item \textbf{K=4 extreme-heterogeneity PR} (\texttt{k4\_pr\_nail}
        floors at $2.6\times10^{-3}$): whether it nails fully under the
        consistent co-area operator is open --- the natural next
        economics result.
  \item \textbf{A cusp-tracking surface representation}: the lookup-side
        segmentation idea lifted to the surface itself (multi-element
        cube patches bounded by the critical manifold) --- the
        principled route to exponential convergence of $P$, beyond the
        scope of the present cycle.
\end{enumerate}
