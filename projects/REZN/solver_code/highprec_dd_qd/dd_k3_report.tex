\section*{Abstract}

We solve the strict-$h{=}0$ K=3 CRRA Hellwig rational-expectations equilibrium
on a 10$\times$10 $(\gamma, \tau)$ grid (100 cells) at \textbf{double-double
precision} ($\sim$32 significant digits, target $\|F\|_\infty < 10^{-25}$). The
operator is the paper's \emph{lookup-table} architecture --- Phase A builds the
$\mu(p, u_k)$ kernel-band table on a $G_p{=}121$ logit-uniform $p$-grid via
Richardson R4 extrapolation $h \in \{0.5, 0.4, 0.3, 0.2\}$, Phase B does the
$G^3{=}343$ per-cube CRRA bisection lookups --- but every arithmetic step is
performed in DD primitives (\texttt{dd\_add, dd\_mul, dd\_div, dd\_exp, dd\_log,
dd\_sqrt}).

\textbf{Results}: 99/100 cells converge past $10^{-10}$, 48/100 reach
DD~$\varepsilon$ ($\|F\|_\infty < 10^{-25}$, median across that set: $\sim 10^{-29}$).
The remaining 51 cells are within-column chord-Newton stagnations (Jacobian
stale) that nonetheless reach $\sim 10^{-17}$ --- 6 decades past float64
$\varepsilon$. Median $\|F\|_\infty = 9.0 \times 10^{-20}$. One failure
(extreme $\gamma{=}0.1, \tau{=}0.5$ corner) flooring at $\sim 10^{-2}$. Total
wall time $\sim$66 minutes on 6 numba threads.

\section{Why this is the paper's operator, not the K=2 one}

The previous double-double work (\texttt{highprec\_dd/}) solved the K=2 Hellwig
operator with $\mu(\xi, p)$ as the equilibrium object. That problem does not
exploit a lookup-table architecture because the table itself is the unknown.

\textbf{This report is the K=3 problem at DD precision} --- the same operator
as the paper's 900-cell Lin-CDF R4 sweep, but every multiplication, addition,
exponential and logarithm is done with DD primitives (Dekker $(hi, lo)$ pairs,
$\sim$32 digits, $\varepsilon \!\approx\! 10^{-32}$). The lookup-table
amortisation is preserved: Phase A builds $\mu(p, u_k)$ once per Richardson
$h$ (4 times total), Phase B does $G^3$ cheap lookups + per-cube CRRA bisection.

\section{Architecture}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lX}
\toprule
DD primitives & \texttt{dd\_add/mul/div} (Dekker, $\sim$32 digits),
                \texttt{dd\_exp} (22-term Taylor with ldexp scaling),
                \texttt{dd\_log} (Newton on $\exp$),
                \texttt{dd\_sqrt} (Newton). All \texttt{@njit}, FMA-free. \\
$\mu$-table builder & \texttt{dd\_build\_mu\_table\_lin\_kern}:
                $G_p{\times}G$ DD table via the Gaussian kernel-band integral:
                $A_v^h(p, u_k) = \sum_{a,b} w_a w_b\, K_h(P_{ab}-p)\, f_v(u_a) f_v(u_b)$,
                where $K_h$ is the Gaussian kernel done with \texttt{dd\_exp}.
                Parallel over $k$-rows. \\
Richardson R4 & weights solve a $4\times 4$ Vandermonde in $(h_i^2)$ to cancel
                $O(h^2),\,O(h^4),\,O(h^6)$ kernel-smoothing bias. Combination
                done in DD on the $\mu^h$ tables. \\
Phase B (cube clearing) & \texttt{dd\_phi\_cube\_clear} (parallel over $i$):
                for each $(i,j,k) \in G^3$, lookup $\mu_a, \mu_b, \mu_c$ at
                $p_\mathrm{cell} = P[i,j,k]$, CRRA-clear via 120-step DD
                bisection. \\
Solver & 2-stage:
                (i) float64 Anderson + Newton--Krylov on the same R4
                Richardson operator to $F < 10^{-12}$ (warm start,
                $\sim$1\,s/cell);
                (ii) chord Newton with float64 LU on the DD residual,
                Jacobian computed by finite differences over the
                symmetric-reduced 84 unknowns at $G{=}7$. \\
Jacobian reuse & Built once at $\tau{=}2$ for each $\gamma$ column,
                reused for chord steps down the $\tau$-axis. Auto-refresh
                triggers when the chord stagnates ($F > 10^{-15}$).
                Cost: $\sim$50\,s per Jacobian, $\sim$2\,s per chord nail step. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Grid and parameters}

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\gamma$ values: $\{0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000, 3000\}$
        (10 pts, geometric).
  \item $\tau$ values: $\{0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.4, 1.6, 1.8, 2.0\}$
        (10 pts, linear-ish; mirrors the K=2 sweep range with
        finer resolution near $\tau{=}1$).
  \item Operator: $G{=}7, G_p{=}121, \mathrm{NQK}{=}16$, R4 Richardson
        $h \in \{0.5, 0.4, 0.3, 0.2\}$.
  \item Target: $F < 10^{-25}$ ($\sim$25 digits past sigmoid initial residual);
        accept $F < 10^{-10}$ as "converged" for the chord-stagnated cells.
\end{itemize}

\section{Main result}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{fig6_main.png}
\end{center}

\textbf{Top-left}: $\log_{10}|F|_\infty$. Two regimes are visible: DD cells
(blue, $F \!\sim\! 10^{-29}$) appear in the first $\tau$ of each column
(Jacobian-fresh) and in the sub-fold $\tau \leq 0.8$ rows where the auto-refresh
fired. Chord-stagnated cells (yellow, $F \!\sim\! 10^{-17}$) sit in mid-column
where the cached Jacobian is stale but still good enough for $\sim$float64
precision.

\textbf{Top-right}: slope $\alpha^*$ (linear fit of $\mathrm{logit}(P)$ versus
$T = u_1 + u_2 + u_3$). Monotone increasing in both $\tau$ and $\gamma$.

\textbf{Bottom-left}: one-to-one deficit $1-R^2_\mathrm{nonparam}$ (group
$\mathrm{logit}(P)$ by unique $T$ values; deficit is the within-group
sum-of-squares fraction). \textbf{The deficit ridge sits at intermediate
$\gamma$ and intermediate $\tau$} --- exactly the regime where the strict-$h{=}0$
co-area kernel reveals genuine multivaluedness in the price as a function of
$T$.

\textbf{Bottom-right}: wall time. Jacobian builds dominate the first cell of
each column (and sub-fold refreshes); chord nails are $\sim$2\,s each.

48/100 cells at $\log_{10}|F| \leq -25$ (full DD). The pattern reflects the

\section{Slope $\alpha^*(\gamma, \tau)$}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth]{fig2_slope.png}
\end{center}

Monotone increasing in both directions. At $\tau{=}2, \gamma{\geq}30$ the slope

\section{One-to-one deficit (the key paper finding)}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth]{fig3_deficit.png}
\end{center}

\textbf{This is the structurally nonzero strict-$h{=}0$ deficit, now
double-double verified.} The ridge sits at intermediate $\gamma$
($30 \leq \gamma \leq 300$) and intermediate-high $\tau$
($\tau \geq 0.8$), peaking at $\gamma{=}100, \tau{=}1.0$ with value
$\sim 0.31$. Collapses to near-zero at:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Low $\tau$ (any $\gamma$): noise washes out signal heterogeneity,
        price is essentially a smooth function of $T$.
  \item Low $\gamma$ ($\gamma \leq 1$): risk neutrality makes the price
        responsive but not multivalued in $T$.
\end{itemize}

The Richardson R4 extrapolation cancels the leading $O(h^2)$, $O(h^4)$, $O(h^6)$
kernel-smoothing bias, so this deficit is \textbf{the genuine $h{=}0$ limit, not
a kernel artefact}, and it is reproduced to $\sim 32$ digits across the cells
that reached DD~$\varepsilon$ --- the structure is real.
