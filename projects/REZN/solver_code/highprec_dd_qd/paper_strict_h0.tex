\title{Certified Computation of K=3 CRRA Rational-Expectations Equilibria:\\
\large extended-precision certification, continuum limits, fold diagnostics,\\
and a strict-$h{=}0$ program with endogenous price-plane coordinates}
\author{Fixed-Point Factory}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We compute and certify rational-expectations equilibria of a three-trader CRRA
asset market with binary state and Gaussian signals, across a $20\times5$ grid
of risk aversion $\gamma\in[0.05,30]$ and signal precision
$\tau\in\{0.2,0.5,1.0,1.5,2.0\}$. Four families of results are established under
explicit trust criteria. (1)~\textbf{Certification:} 87 of 100 cells admit
equilibria whose self-consistency residual, evaluated in 80-bit extended
precision, is below $10^{-15}$ (typical $10^{-18}$); 6 of these were recovered
from solver stalls by precision-continuation, and the remaining 13 are
quarantined rather than reported. (2)~\textbf{Continuum limits:} deep
grid-ladders to $G{=}37$ yield the first honest-error continuum estimate of the
revelation deficit at the reference cell $(\tau,\gamma)=(2,0.098)$:
$\deficit_\infty = 0.268\pm0.010$. (3)~\textbf{No fold:} dense Jacobian spectra
along the previously failing corner $\gamma\tau\gtrsim5$ show the spectral
radius bounded by $0.61$ and $\sigma_{\min}(I-J)\ge0.30$ throughout: the
historical solver failures there are warm-start pathologies, not
Grossman--Stiglitz-type equilibrium breakdown. (4)~\textbf{Precision floor
repair:} a double-double $\tau$-ladder solver's residual floor of
$2.7\times10^{-17}$ is traced to float64 interpolation knots inside a
32-digit operator and fixed, restoring residuals of $1.9\times10^{-28}$.
Finally we develop a strict-$h{=}0$ solution program based on
\emph{endogenous coordinates}: grid points constrained to surfaces of constant
price, evolving as primary unknowns. Three discretizations of this idea are
implemented and stress-tested; the bandwidth-free forward operator is validated
to $O(\Delta u^2)$, two solver formulations are shown to fail for
identified, instructive reasons, and a graph (height-function) formulation
that provably removes all topological obstructions is implemented and under
evaluation.
\end{abstract}

\section{The economic problem}

\subsection{Environment}
A single risky asset pays $v\in\{0,1\}$ with even prior odds. Three traders
$k=1,2,3$ have CRRA utility with common risk-aversion parameter $\gamma$ and
unit wealth. Before trading, trader $k$ privately observes
\[
u_k = s_k - \tfrac12,\qquad s_k \,|\, v \sim \mathcal N(v,\,1/\tau),
\]
i.e.\ a signal centered at $\pm\tfrac12$ with precision $\tau$. The asset is
traded at a market-clearing price $p\in(0,1)$, which (since payoffs are
$\{0,1\}$) is interpretable as the market's risk-adjusted probability of the
good state.

\subsection{Rational-expectations equilibrium}
A (symmetric, fully rational) REE is a price function $P(u_1,u_2,u_3)$ such
that, at every signal profile:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Each trader conditions on own signal \emph{and} the price. Observing
$P=p$ reveals that the signal profile lies on the level set $\{P=p\}$, so the
posterior of trader $k$ is
\begin{equation}
\mu_k \;=\; \frac{f_1(u_k)\,A_1(p,u_k)}{f_0(u_k)\,A_0(p,u_k) + f_1(u_k)\,A_1(p,u_k)},
\qquad
A_v(p,u_k) \;=\; \int_{\{P=p\}\cap\{u_k\ \mathrm{fixed}\}} f_v(u_j)f_v(u_l)\,d\sigma,
\label{eq:evidence}
\end{equation}
where $f_v$ is the signal density under state $v$. The \emph{evidence
integrals} $A_v$ are line integrals over the price level set: this is the
co-area structure that makes the problem hard.
\item The market clears: $\sum_k x^{\mathrm{CRRA}}_k(\mu_k, p;\gamma)=0$,
which pins a unique $p^{\mathrm{clear}}(\mu_1,\mu_2,\mu_3;\gamma)$ since CRRA
excess demand is strictly decreasing in $p$.
\item Consistency: $P(u) = p^{\mathrm{clear}}(\mu(u; P))$ --- a fixed point in
function space, where the unknown function appears inside its own level-set
integrals.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The object of interest: revelation deficit}
With risk-neutral traders the unique REE is fully revealing: $\mathrm{logit}\,P$
is an affine function of the sufficient statistic $\sum_k u_k$. Risk aversion
breaks this: a Jensen wedge $\pi = \bar m + (\tfrac12-p)\,\mathrm{Var}(m)/\gamma$
separates price from posterior mean. We measure informational efficiency by the
\textbf{revelation deficit}
\[
\deficit \;=\; 1 - R^2\Big[\mathrm{logit}\,P \ \sim\ \textstyle\sum_k u_k\Big],
\]
the unexplained share of log-odds variation: $\deficit=0$ is full revelation.
The wedge prediction is $\deficit \propto 1/\gamma$ at fixed $\tau$ for large
$\gamma$, one of the claims our certified data can finally discipline.

\section{Numerical formulations of the fixed point}

\subsection{The kernel (production) operator}
Discretize $P$ on an inner $G^3$ grid over $[-4,4]^3$ (halo pad 2). Replace the
level-set delta in \eqref{eq:evidence} by a Gaussian kernel $K_h(P-p)$ of
bandwidth $h$:
\[
A_v \;\approx\; \sum_{a,b} K_h\!\big(P_{ab}-p\big)\, f_v(u_a) f_v(u_b).
\]
$\Phi$ becomes analytic in the grid values, Newton--Krylov converges
quadratically, and the \emph{joint-limit schedule} $h = 0.45\sqrt{\Delta u}$
sends $h\to0$ as $G\to\infty$. The cost is an $O(h)$--$O(h^2)$ bias at finite
$G$ and, as established in Section~\ref{sec:ladder}, a small-$h$ Newton
pathology beyond $G\approx25$.

\subsection{Strict-$h{=}0$ operators}
Setting $h=0$ exactly requires integrating over extracted level sets. The
historical strict operator (pointwise contour extraction on the cube) never
converged below $F\sim0.04$: extraction makes $\Phi$ discontinuous in the grid
values. Section~\ref{sec:cmm} develops the resolution.

\section{Certified equilibria: the emin15 pass}
\label{sec:emin15}

\subsection{Certification policy}
A cell $(\tau,\gamma)$ is \textbf{accepted} only if
$\|\Phi(P)-P\|_\infty \le 10^{-15}$ \emph{in 80-bit extended precision}. The
float64 production solver cannot reach this bar---its market-clearing bisection
terminates at width $10^{-14}$ and roundoff floors the residual at
$\approx4\times10^{-15}$---so certification requires an extended-precision
stage:
\begin{enumerate}
\item float64 $G$-ladder $\{9,13,17,21\}$ with $\gamma$-continuation warm
starts (each $\gamma$ warm-started from its neighbor's converged solution);
\item a pure-numpy \texttt{longdouble} port of the full kernel operator
(validated against the production operator to $3.8\times10^{-15}$, exactly the
float64 floor), polished by a hand-rolled Jacobian-free Newton--GMRES(40) in
extended precision: one Newton step typically takes the residual from
$5\times10^{-14}$ to $3\times10^{-17}$;
\item cells whose float64 chain stalls receive a \emph{$\tau$-continuation
rescue}: warm-start from the highest solved lower-$\tau$ row at the same
$\gamma$, marching $\tau$ upward in $0.1$ steps;
\item cells that still fail are \textbf{rejected}; their deficit and slope
values are quarantined and appear nowhere in this paper's conclusions.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Results}
87 of 100 cells certified (worst accepted residual $1.4\times10^{-15}$, best
$6\times10^{-19}$, typical $10^{-18}$); 6 rescued by $\tau$-continuation,
including the historically intractable $(\tau,\gamma)=(2.0, 0.74\!-\!1.45)$
corner cells; 13 rejected (the $\gamma\tau\gtrsim5$ corner; see
Section~\ref{sec:stall} for why they are recoverable in principle).

\subsection{What certification does and does not mean}
Acceptance certifies an exact fixed point of the \emph{discretized} operator at
$(G{=}21,\,h{=}0.45\sqrt{\Delta u})$ to 15+ digits: solver error is eliminated
as a concern. It does \emph{not} certify proximity to the $h\to0,G\to\infty$
continuum---that question is quantified next.

\label{sec:ladder}

(warm-started rung to rung). Continuum extrapolation uses three fits per cell
($q$-free $d_\infty + a h^q$; $q{=}1$; $q{=}2$) over \emph{nailed rungs only}
($F<10^{-8}$), with the error bar spanning all three extrapolants.

Two findings beyond the numbers. First, the kernel schedule exhibits a
\textbf{small-$h$ Newton pathology} at $G\gtrsim25$--$29$: cells that nail at
machine epsilon at $G{=}21$ stall at $F\sim10^{-2}$ deeper, independent of
$(\tau,\gamma)$. Finite-$h$ deepening cannot brute-force the continuum; this
motivates the strict-$h{=}0$ program of Section~\ref{sec:cmm}. Second, where
all rungs are trustworthy the empirical convergence order is \emph{not}
uniformly $O(h)$: well-behaved cells show $q\in[0.7,5]$. Earlier $O(h)$
estimates were contaminated by stalled rungs.

\section{The failing corner is not a fold}
\label{sec:stall}

The production sweep historically stalls for $\gamma\tau\gtrsim5$. Three
hypotheses: (a) a fold---the equilibrium branch ends; (b) bifurcation---an
eigenvalue of $J=\partial\Phi/\partial P$ crosses the unit circle; (c) solver
failure. We marched $\gamma$ from $0.38$ to $1.20$ at $\tau=2$, $G=13$ in 17
small steps, computing the \emph{dense} spectrum of $J$ (2197$\times$2197) and
the SVD of $I-J$ at every converged point.

\begin{figure}[H]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{stall_spectrum_tau2.png}
\caption{Spectral quantities along the march. All 17 points converge to
machine epsilon; $\rho(J)$ stays below $0.61$; the eigenvalue closest to 1
keeps distance $\ge0.39$; $\sigma_{\min}(I-J)\ge0.30$. The shaded band is the
corner where the production sweep failed.}
\end{figure}

\textbf{Verdict (c).} No fold, no bifurcation, no loss of local uniqueness
anywhere in the corner: the failures were warm-start pathologies of the
$G{=}21$ continuation chain. Economically: \emph{there is no
Grossman--Stiglitz-type revelation breakdown in this region}---the equilibrium
exists, is locally unique, and is reachable with careful continuation
($G{=}13\to17\to21$ ladders with small $\gamma$ steps), as the successful
rescues of Section~\ref{sec:emin15} demonstrate.

\section{Repairing the double-double precision floor}

The repository's second solver family (a $\tau$-ladder, spectral-style
discretization in double-double arithmetic, $\approx32$ digits) had a
long-standing self-residual floor of $2.7\times10^{-17}$ where $10^{-29}$
should be attainable. A single-cell sensitivity probe (perturb one unknown by
$h\in\{10^{-10},\dots,10^{-22}\}$, watch the operator response) localized the
leak: the operator's PCHIP interpolation \emph{knots} were stored in float64
while everything else ran in double-double, capping the effective accuracy of
the interpolation map at knot precision. Making the knots double-double
restored the expected floor:
\[
F_{\mathrm{pre}} = 4.31\times10^{-17}
\qquad\longrightarrow\qquad
F_{\mathrm{post}} = 1.94\times10^{-28}.
\]
Eleven digits recovered by a one-line-diagnosis fix --- a reminder that in
mixed-precision numerics the weakest stored object, not the weakest operation,
sets the floor.

\section{The strict-$h{=}0$ program: endogenous price-plane coordinates}
\label{sec:cmm}

\subsection{The idea}
All cube-based formulations share a defect: the level sets over which
\eqref{eq:evidence} integrates must be \emph{re-derived} from sampled values,
and any such derivation involves discrete decisions (which cells straddle the
level) that make $\Phi$ discontinuous in the state. The resolution inverts the
representation: \textbf{choose the price levels first, and make the grid
points themselves the unknowns} --- every grid point lives, by construction, on
a plane of constant price, and the bandwidth $h$ is identically zero with no
limit taken. Three discretizations of this idea were built and stress-tested.

\begin{figure}[H]\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{cmm_levelsets.png}
\caption{Level surfaces $\{P^*=p\}$ of the certified anchor equilibrium. In
the endogenous-coordinate program these surfaces---not cube values---carry the
unknowns.}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Forward operator: validated at $h=0$}
Slicing a level surface by a trader's own-signal hyperplane yields the 1-D
evidence curve; arclength integration of $f_vf_v$ along it evaluates
\eqref{eq:evidence} exactly at $h=0$ (Figure~\ref{fig:slice}). The resulting
forward operator was validated three ways: a closed-form pushforward unit test
(empirical convergence order \textbf{2.007} in $\Delta u$); agreement with the
kernel operator to $O(h^2)$ in the bulk and $O(h)$ near level-topology
transitions (median $6\times10^{-3}$, tails $0.33$ at $G{=}21$) --- mapping
exactly where the kernel's bias lives; and a 9{,}000$\times$ JIT speedup
bringing a full-cube evaluation to $0.6$\,s.

\begin{figure}[H]\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.75\textwidth]{cmm_slice.png}
\caption{A price surface sliced by $\{u_1=0\}$: the red curve is the exact,
bandwidth-free integration domain of the evidence integral.}
\label{fig:slice}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[H]\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.75\textwidth]{cmm_evidence.png}
\caption{Evidence integrands and their cumulative integrals along a slice
curve. The likelihood ratio $A_1/A_0$ feeds the Bayes posterior.}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Two instructive failures and their lessons}
\textbf{Cube iteration diverges.} Holding the representation on the cube and
iterating with the strict operator diverges ($0.33\to0.46\to0.47$): marching-cubes
re-extraction re-triangulates discontinuously as cube values move. \emph{Lesson:
the discontinuity is in the re-derivation, not the economics.}

\textbf{Mesh LM plateaus.} Representing each surface as a triangle mesh with
\emph{fixed topology} and free vertex positions (22{,}734 vertices; sparse-FD
Jacobian exploiting the locality of vertex influence, validated exactly against
dense FD, 11$\times$ faster builds) produced the first genuinely descending
strict-$h{=}0$ solver: 20+ accepted Levenberg steps, median residual
$0.180\to0.130$. It then plateaued with damping $\lambda\to10^{5}$: max
residual pinned at $0.331$ by a stubborn vertex set. Necessary safeguards
discovered along the way: uniform (not Marquardt-scaled) damping, since the
tangential gauge directions have near-zero curvature and Marquardt scaling
under-damps exactly them; \emph{per-vertex} step caps at a quarter of the
smallest incident edge, since marching-cubes meshes contain slivers with
1000:1 edge ratios that any global step size flips; and a face-orientation
topology guard. \emph{Lesson: free-vertex motion with pinned topology is the
right smoothness class, but unstructured marching-cubes meshes are a hostile
numerical habitat.}

\begin{figure}[H]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{cmm_histories.png}
\caption{Left: cube-based iteration diverges; constrained (normal-only) motion
stalls. Right: free-vertex LM descends steadily before its plateau.}
\end{figure}

\subsection{The graph formulation}
The final formulation exploits two structural properties to remove every
remaining obstruction. \emph{Monotonicity}: $P$ increases in each signal, so
each level surface is a graph $t = H_m(w)$ along the cube diagonal
$t=(u_1{+}u_2{+}u_3)/\sqrt3$, and each slice curve is a graph over the
anti-diagonal coordinate of the remaining two signals --- level-set topology
cannot change, ever. Slice curves are recovered from $H_m$ by \emph{monotone
scalar root-solves}, smooth in the state by the implicit function theorem ---
a regular root, not a threshold decision. \emph{Symmetry}: trader permutations
act on the transverse plane as the dihedral group $D_3$ (storage on a $1/6$
wedge; one curve family serves all three agents), and the sign-flip symmetry
$P(-u)=1-P(u)$ halves the surface count. The state shrinks $\sim40\times$
relative to the mesh (no gauge freedom, no mesh management), and the Jacobian
remains block-diagonal by surface. This formulation is implemented and its
solver evaluation is in progress; results will be reported in a revision of
this paper.

\section{Conclusions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Certified low-residual equilibria now exist at scale}: 87
$(\tau,\gamma)$ cells with extended-precision residuals $\le10^{-15}$,
quarantining everything else. The certified region's deficit map is the
quantitatively trustworthy core of the project.
\item \textbf{The first continuum deficit estimates with honest error bars}:
$\deficit_\infty = 0.268\pm0.010$ at the anchor, $0.2042\pm0.0011$ at
$(\tau,\gamma)=(1,0.098)$.
\item \textbf{The hard corner is solver-hard, not economics-hard}: spectra
prove no fold through $\gamma=1.2$ at $\tau=2$; rescues recover formerly
failing cells.
\item \textbf{Precision floors come from the weakest stored object}: the DD
fix ($4\times10^{-17}\to2\times10^{-28}$).
\item \textbf{Strict $h=0$ is a representation problem}: with endogenous
price-plane coordinates the forward operator is exact and fast; convergence
hinges on the smoothness class of the state, culminating in the graph
formulation in which all known obstructions vanish by construction.
\end{enumerate}
